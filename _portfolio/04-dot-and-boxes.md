* **Developed** a playable Dot and Boxes implementation supporting human-versus-AI matches.
* **Implemented** minimax search with Alpha-Beta Pruning, cutting branches that cannot influence the final decision so the agent searches meaningfully deeper within the same time budget.
* **Optimized** the AI's decision-making for a game whose branching factor is high early and whose endgame is decided by chain parity — a setting where search depth translates directly into playing strength.
